# Licensing Policy

This repository is a multi-project research workspace. There is **no blanket open-source license** covering every experiment or branch.

Each project or benchmark branch must carry its own explicit license before third parties may reuse, redistribute, sublicense, commercialize, or create derivative works from its original content.

If a branch/project does not contain an explicit license, the original portions of that branch/project are **All Rights Reserved** by their applicable copyright holder(s).

Third-party libraries, models, datasets, assets, generated materials, and other third-party components remain subject to their own licenses and terms. Nothing in this policy overrides those rights.

For experiments that are intentionally released as open source, use a project-specific OSI-approved license in that branch and document the scope clearly in its README.
